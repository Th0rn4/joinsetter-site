Questions

Before you book

Do I have to change my booking tool?

No. Setter works around what you already use, whether that's Calendly, a form on your site or an application page.

How long does setup take?

About a week from our first call. You'll spend roughly an hour of that answering questions and approving the messages.

Will the messages sound like me?

Yes. Every message is written in your voice and approved by you before anything goes live.

What do I actually have to do?

Take the calls. Setter handles replying, reminding, rebooking and following up.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month, and you can cancel any month.

Who's behind Setter?

Me, Reuben. I build and run every system myself, so you deal with the person doing the work.
